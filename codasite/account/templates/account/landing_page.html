The Reality

Traditional site management relies on trust. Trust is expensive.

Ghost Workers

Are you paying day laborers who never showed up? Paper sign-in sheets are easily faked by proxy. If you aren't geofencing them, you are losing money.

Material Theft

100 bags of cement arrive. Only 80 are used. Where did the other 20 go? If your supervisor writes it down in a notebook, the data is already dead.

The Solution

The GPS QR Gate Lock
